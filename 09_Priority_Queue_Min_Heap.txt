TITLE: 9. MIN HEAP INSERTION AND DELETION
AIM: Create a min heap and delete its minimum element.

ALGORITHM:
1. Start.
2. Import the heapq module.
3. Create an empty heap.
4. Insert 30, 10, 20, and 5 one by one using heappush.
5. Display the heap.
6. Remove the smallest element using heappop.
7. Display the deleted value and the updated heap.
8. Stop.

PROGRAM:
import heapq

heap = []
for value in [30, 10, 20, 5]:
    heapq.heappush(heap, value)

print("Min Heap:", *heap)
minimum = heapq.heappop(heap)
print("Deleted Minimum =", minimum)
print("Heap after Deletion:", *heap)

OUTPUT:
Min Heap: 5 10 20 30
Deleted Minimum = 5
Heap after Deletion: 10 30 20

RESULT: A min heap was created and its minimum element was deleted successfully.
